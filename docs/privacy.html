Privacy Policy — UT Reserve

Last updated: 21 August 2026

The short version. UT Reserve does not collect, store, or share your personal information. Your UT EID and password are typed into UT Austin's own login page and are never seen by this app. Nothing that identifies you ever leaves your device.

UT Reserve is an independent app built by a student for browsing and booking UT Austin RecSports courts. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by The University of Texas at Austin. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

1.Your UT credentials

The app signs you in by loading UT's own login page — Shibboleth single sign-on, followed by Duo two-factor — inside an embedded browser view. You type your UT EID and password into UT's page, and they are transmitted directly to UT. The app does not read, log, store, or transmit your credentials. There is no server behind this app that could receive them.

Your signed-in session is held as an HttpOnly cookie in your device's system browser storage. HttpOnly means the app's own code is technically prevented from reading it. Signing out navigates to UT's logout endpoint, which ends the session with UT itself rather than merely forgetting it locally.

2.What is stored on your device

  • A single flag recording that you have signed in before, so the app can skip the login screen. Stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Your reservations, held in memory only while the app is open and re-read from UT's website each time. They are never written to disk and never sent anywhere.
  • Any reminders you schedule. These are local notifications generated and delivered entirely by your own device. There is no push server and no push token.

3.What is sent off your device

Exactly one kind of data: court availability.

When you check a facility, the app reads that facility's availability grid from UT's reservation site using your signed-in session, and saves the result to a shared database (hosted by Supabase) so that other users of the app can see which courts are open. Each record contains only:

  • the facility's ID and name
  • the date that was checked
  • the list of open court and time slots
  • a timestamp

No user identifier, account, device ID, or reservation of yours is included. These records describe courts, not people. There is no way to determine from the database who checked a facility, or who holds a booking.

The app also reads facility opening hours from that same database. Those hours are collected from UT RecSports' publicly available hours page by an automated job. No user data is involved.

4.What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No crash-reporting or performance-monitoring SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone
  • No accounts, profiles, or sign-up — the app has no user database
  • No access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data

5.Third-party services

  • Supabase hosts the shared court-availability database. As with any web service, Supabase's infrastructure may record standard operational request logs, which can include IP addresses, for security and reliability purposes. Those logs are not used by this app to identify, profile, or track anyone.
  • The University of Texas at Austin operates the reservation website the app loads. Your use of that site is governed by UT's own policies, not this one.

6.Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Using it requires a UT EID.

7.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
