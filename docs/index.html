UT Reserve

Support & help

UT Reserve is a faster way to browse and book UT Austin RecSports courts from your phone — squash, racquetball, pickleball and tennis across all eight facilities.

What you need

  • A valid UT EID, and the ability to complete Duo two-factor sign-in
  • iOS 16 or later

The app books through UT's own reservation system, so anything you book here appears in your RecSports account exactly as if you had booked it on the website — and vice versa.

Common questions

Why do I have to sign in through UT's own page?

Because that is the only safe way to do it. The app never sees your EID or password — you type them into UT's real login page, and the app has no server that could receive them. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Why does the "open now" section sometimes show nothing?

Availability shown on the home screen comes from other students' checks, not from a constant background scan of UT's servers. If nobody has checked a facility recently, there is nothing to show. Open the Courts tab and pick a facility to get live availability.

It says I already have a reservation, but I don't.

UT allows one reservation per day per person, and enforces it on their end. A court you already played today can still count against that limit even though it no longer appears in your upcoming reservations. The message shown is UT's own.

How do I cancel?

Tap Cancel on the reservation, either on the home screen or in My Reservations. This cancels it with UT directly and also removes any reminder you scheduled for it.

Are the facility hours always right?

Hours are collected daily from UT RecSports' published hours page, and the app shows which period they cover and when they were last updated. During breaks and holidays UT sometimes changes hours without updating that page — treat the hours as a good guide, not a guarantee.

How do I sign out?

My Reservations tab, top right. This ends your session with UT, not just inside the app, so signing back in requires your EID and password again.
